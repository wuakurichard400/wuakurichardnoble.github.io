Educational Background

My educational journey began at Peki Blengo E.P. School, where I studied from Kindergarten to JHS 3. During my final year in Junior High School, I had the privilege of serving as the Boys' Prefect. I successfully completed and wrote the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).


I continued my education at Bompeh Senior High Technical School, Takoradi, where I studied from 2022 to 2025. During my final academic year, I had the honor of serving as the Protocol Prefect for the 2024/2025 academic year. I successfully completed my secondary education and sat for the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE).


Currently, I am pursuing a degree in Computer Science (Software Engineering) at Takoradi Technical University (TTU). My studies focus on software engineering, programming, technology, innovation, problem-solving, and the development of modern software solutions.
